# Creative world & character design

Announce once: **Using `creative-world-character-design`**.

Authority: Theme 5 accepted (T5D world/character). Methods vary by medium.  
Run **`design-process`** spine first for non-trivial setting/cast work.

## When to use

- World / setting bible structure and consistency rules
- Characters, cast webs, personality/function constraints

**Out:** Quest/plot structure → `creative-narrative-design`. Systems balance → `creative-systems-design`. Treating TTRPG/GM random tables as video-game systems law. Product UX (T5C).

## World bible (practical shape)

Prefer a living, dated bible with tight facts over kitchen-sink lore:

1. **World** — places, rules of reality (magic/tech **with costs**), factions, history that matters to play
2. **Cast** — who matters; functions in the story/game
3. **Appendices** — only what you will actually use

Anti-pattern: incoherent “everything” settings. Prefer focus (inside-out or outside-in is fine — state which).

## Characters

- Prefer **function + distilled traits + relationships (web) + status** over novel-length bios
- Distinguish **novelist vs game designer vs GM** jobs — do not force one medium’s sheet on another
- TTRPG NPC sketch patterns are optional tools, **not** VG law

## Agent role

- Propose structured bible/cast options; flag contradictions
- Consistency critique (rules broken, kitchen-sink creep)
- Human decides; short quotes only — no copyrighted lore dumps
